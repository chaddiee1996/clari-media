## C5 Peri, meno, post: the words (23 Nov)
1. Three small moons, a crescent, a full moon and a crescent facing the other way, beside the words Perimenopause. Menopause. Postmenopause. Text: What's the difference?
2. A large waxing crescent moon. Perimenopause: when your periods become irregular.
3. A large full moon. Menopause: when you haven't had a period for 12 months, and you're not using hormonal contraception.
4. A large waning crescent moon. Postmenopause: the time after. Some symptoms can carry on, like joint pain and vaginal dryness.
5. An age ruler from 40 to 60 with 45 to 55 highlighted in pink. Text: It usually happens between 45 and 55.
6. An age ruler from 35 to 50 with small moons at 40 and 45. Text: Early menopause: before 45. Premature menopause: before 40.
7. A crescent moon. Text: There's no routine test for it. A doctor will usually go by your age and symptoms.
8. The Clari logo and a blossom branch. Text: Save this. Send it to her. Source: NHS.
