When the NHS says to contact your GP.

1

You think it could be perimenopause or menopause, and want to know your options.

2

Symptoms like a fast heartbeat (palpitations).

3

You still have periods, but you're bleeding more, not less, than before.

4

You haven't had a period for 12 months or more, and then get any vaginal bleeding.

Early advice can help reduce the effects on your health, relationships and work.
